## Value Proposition

- **Emotional value**: Pure nostalgia and affection. Every "naughty" moment is reframed as lovable, memorable personality — a comic the family can treasure and Muiju can read when she is older.
- **Laugh value**: Villain-origin parody escalated to mock-Marvel scale — an innocent baby revealed as a reincarnated master spy who becomes a superpower villain is inherently funny. Comic exaggeration of real antics (midnight self-delivery, diaper sprays, milk artillery, framed theft, COVID innocence, rash sabotage).
- **Practical value**: No instruction needed — this is a celebration. Works as a gift/keepsake and as a family inside-joke artifact.

## Core Message

"Every family has a ruler, and ours is a one-and-a-half-year-old reincarnated superspy turned supervillain Empress — and we wouldn't have it any other way."

## Recommended Approach

A **comedic supervillain-origin biography in 62 pages** with cliffhangers at every even-numbered page — chronologically organized acts:

1. **Act 0 — Womb & Arrival (1-6)**: Impatient self-delivery at midnight; innocent arrival home; the reincarnated spy reveal.
2. **Act I — Wet Crusades (7-12)**: Diaper-change spray warfare; parents blamed themselves.
3. **Act II — Milk & Mercy (13-18)**: Encore bottles loaded as vomit ammunition targeting Dad.
4. **Act III — The Helper's Fall (19-26)**: At 3 months, framing and firing the neutral helper (planted cotton buds + vitamin C).
5. **Act IV — AWOWO (27-32)**: Age 1 — the first word is a refusal; the sonic voice awakens.
6. **Act V — The Belly Throne (33-38)**: Dad's fat belly as a sleep-throne, softened by head-butts.
7. **Act VI — Midnight Tributes & The Plague (39-47)**: Twice-nightly milk tributes (14-16m); knowingly catching COVID at the playground (15m) and letting Dad take the blame.
8. **Act VII — Food War (48-50)**: Spurning baby food, commandeering the parents' plates (1.5y).
9. **Act VIII — Operation: Cut the Trip (51-56)**: Rash sabotage cuts the Taiwan trip short — forfeiting dinner reservations and Odyssey in IMAX.
10. **Act IX — Powers & the Challenger (57-62)**: The mock-Marvel power awakening, coronation as Empress, and the pregnancy-test cliffhanger finale.

Open-loop structure: ends on a cliffhanger — a challenger is coming to the throne — inviting a sequel.

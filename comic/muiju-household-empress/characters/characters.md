# Character Definitions - Muiju: The Rise of the Household Empress

**Style**: manga
**Art Direction**: Warm slice-of-life manga; clean smooth lines, big expressive anime eyes, chibi-cute proportions for Muiju, soft warm color grading. English dialogue.
**Tone modifiers**: warm (soft contrast, golden light, slightly reduced saturation, cream-white backgrounds)
**Face reference**: `comic/character_faces.png` — facial details below marked (ref) are derived from that image. Grandparents are split by family side: dad-side vs mum-side.

---

## Character 1: Muiju (the "Villain")

**Role**: Protagonist / self-styled Empress
**Age**: 1.5 years old

**Appearance**:
- Face shape: Round, chubby cheeks, full baby face (ref)
- Skin tone: Fair, smooth baby skin (ref)
- Hair: Short, straight black hair in a bowl-cut style with fringe across the forehead (ref)
- Eyebrows: Faint, light black, barely defined (ref)
- Eyes: Very large, dark brown, round and wide-set, sparkly manga eyes — biggest eyes in the cast (ref)
- Nose: Small, button-like, slightly upturned (ref)
- Mouth: Small, natural pink, slightly pouty (ref)
- Build: Chubby, tiny, wobbly toddling gait
- Distinguishing features: Rosy cheeks; occasionally a tiny mischief smirk with one raised eyebrow; an "angel halo" visual motif that appears when she is being sweet
- Asian Chinese

**Costume**:
- Default outfit: Pink short-sleeve t-shirt + pink shorts
- Color palette: Hot pink (#EC4899), soft white, baby-pink accents
- Accessories: Sometimes a small stuffed toy — a white rabbit in an orange soccer jersey and shorts with black soccer boots

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral: Wide innocent eyes, soft open-mouth smile (slightly serious resting face per ref)
- Happy/Excited: Gigantic grin, sparkles around head, arms raised
- Mischief/Cunning: Narrowed eyes, sly smile, little fangs suggestion, shadowed gaze
- Determined: Puffed cheeks, pointed finger, tiny furrowed brows
- Melting-the-adults: Big teary puppy eyes, lower lip pout, arms reaching for a hug

**Visual Reference Notes**: Chibi super-deformed proportions — head ~1/2 to 1/3 of body height. Keep her cuter than cute even when "evil". Her villainy should read as adorable. Face must match `character_faces.png`: bowl-cut black hair with fringe, button nose, huge round dark-brown eyes.
